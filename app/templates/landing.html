THE GAMKERS LEARNING CENTRE

Learn the basics.
Build with confidence.

No videos or outside resources needed. Explore visual guides and practical tips that explain the essentials of creating a useful online form.

GUIDE 01 · PLAN YOUR FORM

Start with a clear purpose

A good form begins with one simple question: what information do you actually need?

What you'll learn
  • Choose one goal for your form.
  • Ask only for information you will use.
  • Group related questions together.
  • Keep the form short and focused.
GUIDE 02 · WRITE QUESTIONS

Make every question easy

Choose an answer format that feels natural and make your wording clear to everyone.

What you'll learn
  • Use short, direct question wording.
  • Use text fields for personal details.
  • Use choices when answers are predictable.
  • Mark only essential questions as required.
GUIDE 03 · PUBLISH & REVIEW

Know what happens next

Preview your form, share it when ready, and review the responses that come in.

What you'll learn
  • Preview your form before publishing.
  • Share the public form link.
  • Review submitted responses in your workspace.
  • Use a clear confirmation message after submission.
